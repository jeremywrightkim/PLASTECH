Company Overview

Plastech service Inc. — a specialist in plastic additives

About Us

Since its founding in 1998, Plastech service Inc. has concentrated on a single field in order to advance the plastics and chemical materials industry. To compensate for the property limits of base resins and bring out their optimal performance, we have reliably supplied flame retardants, heat stabilizers and flow processing aids along with a wide range of high-performance additives to plastic compounding and chemical materials sites across Korea, growing alongside our customers.

That supply capability rests on an official master distributorship with Albemarle and a collaboration system with NAGASE & Co. of Japan. We are also broadening our business into the eco-friendly trend of the petrochemical market and the biodegradable materials market, a global priority in recent years.

As a future growth engine, we are developing a business in high-performance additives and advanced high-heat-dissipation materials essential to the thermal management of devices for home appliances, mobile, semiconductors and eco-friendly vehicles.

Keeping pace with rapidly shifting global materials market trends, we will grow into a core materials leader contributing to the advancement of Korea's high-tech IT and manufacturing industries.

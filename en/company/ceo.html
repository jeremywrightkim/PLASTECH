Thank you for visiting the PLASTECH website.

Since our founding, PLASTECH has reliably supplied high-quality chemical raw materials and advanced materials to a wide range of industries including coating, paint, electronics, display and plastics. We import carefully selected materials from Japan, China, India and Southeast Asia to supply domestic manufacturers, and export outstanding Korean products to the global market, serving as a trusted bridge.

Even in a rapidly changing market, we have upheld our core values of integrity and quality. We will continue to expand into future growth areas such as eco-friendly materials, secondary batteries and OLED, and remain a dependable partner delivering the optimal solutions for our customers.

We sincerely appreciate your continued interest and support. Thank you.

Thank you for visiting the PLASTECH Service website.

Since our founding in 1998, PLASTECH Service has concentrated on a single field: plastic additives. We have grown alongside our customers by reliably supplying flame retardants, heat stabilizers and flow processing aids that compensate for the property limits of base resins and raise their performance, to plastic compounding and chemical materials sites across Korea.

The materials industry is passing through a major transition. The petrochemical market's demand for sustainability leads to biodegradable materials, and the growing density of electronics and mobility devices leads to thermal management materials. Building on our official Albemarle master distributorship and our collaboration with NAGASE & Co. of Japan, we will prepare for this shift one step ahead of our customers.

Honest quality, delivery as promised, and a technically dependable partner. These are the standards we have kept, and the standards we will continue to keep. We sincerely appreciate your continued interest and support. Thank you.

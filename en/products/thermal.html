Heat Dissipation  Cu-Diamond

A copper–diamond composite with high thermal conductivity, applied at the lid (heat spreader) position of a chip package. In the Die → TIM1 → Lid → TIM2 → Cold plate heat path it spreads heat far more effectively than copper, while its low coefficient of thermal expansion stays close to that of the die — which helps interface reliability under repeated thermal cycling.

SAYTEX® ALERO

Albemarle · Polymeric brominated flame retardant

Modern fire safety, advanced sustainability

Albemarle's next-generation multi-purpose polymeric flame retardant. It combines proven recyclability with a broad processing window and tailored material properties, helping manufacturers meet fire safety requirements in an increasingly electrified and digitalised world.

PRODUCT OVERVIEW

A polymeric flame retardant for today's and tomorrow's materials

Requirements differ by base resin. ALERO brings a distinct benefit to each of the main engineering resins.

  • ABS — excellent performance and appearance at lower loadings than competing flame retardants.
  • Polypropylene (PP) — melt-blendable, improving product uniformity and formulation flexibility, and supporting PTFE-free formulations.
  • PC / ABS — strong impact resistance with no synergist required.
